# QuizProof

_Learn web3, prove it on-chain with soulbound knowledge NFTs_

## Summary

QuizProof is a web3 trivia app for beginners covering blockchain history, tech, and current topics. As users answer correctly, they level up and earn soulbound NFTs that certify their knowledge tier, creating a verifiable on-chain learning record.

## Target users

Web3 newcomers who want a fun, gamified way to learn and show off their knowledge

## Problem

Beginners find web3 concepts intimidating and have no easy way to track or prove their learning progress.

## Solution

A bite-sized quiz app that rewards correct answers with XP and mints tiered soulbound NFTs as verifiable proof of knowledge.

## MVP features

- Topic-based quiz categories (history, tech, DeFi, NFTs, security)
- XP and leveling system with difficulty scaling
- Soulbound NFT minted per knowledge tier (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Diamond)
- Daily quiz streak tracker with bonus rewards
- Public profile page showing earned NFTs and quiz stats

## Chains

Solana

## Tech

Next.js, Solana Web3.js, Anchor, Metaplex Core (soulbound NFTs), Supabase, Phantom Wallet Adapter

Consumer

## Why now

Colosseum hackathon draws many newcomers to Solana ecosystem who need accessible onboarding tools, and soulbound NFTs are gaining traction as credential primitives.
